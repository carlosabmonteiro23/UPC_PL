## Deadlines and procedure

The deadline, which cannot be extended, is **25/11/2026**. Applications are online only, through the [UPC e-office form](https://seuelectronica.upc.edu/ca/tots-els-tramits/convocatoria_proces_selectiu_professorat_lector). You need a digital ID (digital certificate, idCAT or Cl@ve), and the €74.96 fee is paid by card in the same form. Only merits held by that date count.

Some 2027 dates appear as "2026" on the website. Read them as 2027 and confirm with concursospdi.saip@upc.edu, subject "Concursos PDI Laboral temporal - Lector".

The competition has two tests:
- **Test 1:** paper evaluation of the HAP and PT, out of 45 points plus preferential merits.
- **Test 2:** public, in-person session of up to 90 minutes, out of 40 points. It covers the HAP defence (≥40%), the PT defence (≥20%) and a lesson on a subject of the profile (≥20%).

The final score is the sum of both tests.

## Scoring criteria

Test 1 is worth 45 points, plus up to 20 for preferential merits. The committee may set a minimum threshold of up to 27/45. Test 2 is worth 40 points, with a threshold of up to 24/40 (base 6.3).

| Criterion (test 1) | Max. | Document | What to show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Teaching experience matching the profile | 10 | HAP | TTC theory, MF labs, Fluid Engineering (spring), hours per subject, certificates |
| 2. Research and transfer experience matching the profile | 10 | HAP | Papers, projects, codes and HPC allocations across the 3 lines |
| 3. Teaching-training courses | 5 | HAP | ICE-UPC courses, with hours and certificates |
| 4. Internationalisation | 5 | HAP | CTR-Stanford 2026, UBC 2027, international co-authors, conferences |
| 5. Other | 5 | HAP | Languages (Catalan courses), management, outreach, awards, reviewing |
| 6. Work plan | 10 | PT | Teaching and research plan matching the profile |
| 7. AQU Lector / ANECA PAD accreditation | +10 | Accreditation | Pass / fail only |
| 8. Excellence postdoc contract of ≥1 year | +10 | Accreditation | Pass / fail only |

The committee publishes its sub-criteria on the call's website once it is constituted. Read them before preparing test 2.

## Academic and Professional Record (HAP)

Free format, no page limit. Follow the examples: about 10–15 pages of record, then the certificates linked from each merit (Royano's HAP is 11 pages plus annexes, 60 in total). **Draft: `HAP/main.tex`, in English, filled from the CV.**

1. **Header:** name and post details, no other personal data.
2. **Education and career:** PhD (UPC, 2023–2026, Excellent cum laude), MSc (NOVA Lisbon), Erasmus (Politecnico di Milano), postdoc and industry experience.
3. **Teaching** (criterion 1): TTC theory 2026–27, MF labs since 2024–25, Fluid Engineering assigned for spring, TFG co-supervision.
4. **Teaching training** (criterion 3): currently the ICE course *M2.4 – Atenea: Curs de nivell mitjà* (3 h, 9–11/11/2026). Request the certificate as soon as it ends and add other ICE courses that finish before 25/11 (see below).
5. **Research** (criterion 2): 7 papers, 2 conference papers, 2 datasets and 6 HPC allocations, organised by the 3 lines.
6. **Internationalisation** (criterion 4): CTR 2026, UBC 2027, collaborations.
7. **Other merits** (criterion 5): languages, industry experience.
8. **Career narrative** (one page).
9. **Fit to the profile:** table linking each subject and line to its merits.
10. **Annex:** certificates in order of citation (folder `HAP/documents_justificacio/`).
